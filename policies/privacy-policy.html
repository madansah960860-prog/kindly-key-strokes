Privacy Policy

By the Kindly Keystrokes Editorial Team · Last reviewed: October 2026

Effective date: October 4, 2026. The following privacy policy governs your use of Kindly Keystrokes.

Your privacy matters to us at Kindly Keystrokes, and the simplest way to protect it is to collect almost nothing — which is exactly what we do. This page explains, in plain language, what little we handle and the choices you have.

What we collect

Kindly Keystrokes does not require you to create an account, and it does not run analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts of any kind. The only personal information we ever receive is the information you choose to send us — for example, the content of an email you write to hello@kindlykeystrokes.com.

When you email Kindly Keystrokes, we see your email address and whatever you include in your message. We read it only to answer you, and we do not add you to any mailing list.

Preferences stored on your device

Kindly Keystrokes saves two small preferences in your browser's own local storage: the text size you pick with the "Text size" control, and whether you have dismissed the storage-notice bar. These stay on your device, are never sent to us or anyone else, and you can clear them at any time through your browser settings.

We do not sell or share your information

Kindly Keystrokes does not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information with anyone. There is no advertising network, no third-party pixel, and no social login anywhere on kindlykeystrokes.com.

Your privacy rights (CCPA / CPRA)

California residents have specific rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act. Kindly Keystrokes honors these rights for every reader, wherever you live:

  • The right to know what personal information is collected, used, or disclosed.
  • The right to delete personal information we hold about you.
  • The right to correct inaccurate personal information.
  • The right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information — which does not apply here, because we do nothing of the kind.
  • The right to limit the use of sensitive personal information.
  • The right not to be treated differently for exercising any of these rights.

Because the only personal information we ever hold is an email you sent us, exercising most of these rights is as simple as writing to hello@kindlykeystrokes.com and asking us to delete that message.

Children's privacy (COPPA)

Kindly Keystrokes is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has sent us personal information, please write to hello@kindlykeystrokes.com and we will delete it promptly.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Kindly Keystrokes handles information, we will update this page and change the effective date above before the change takes effect. We encourage you to glance at this page now and then.

Contact us

Questions about privacy can go to hello@kindlykeystrokes.com. We aim to reply within two business days.

Kindly Keystrokes

Kindly Keystrokes is an independent educational website. We publish free, patient lessons for people getting started with computers and email. We sell nothing and offer no services.
