About Kindly Keystrokes

By the Kindly Keystrokes Editorial Team · Last reviewed: October 2026

Kindly Keystrokes exists for one reason: to make a first computer feel friendly instead of frightening. We write for adults who are meeting computers later in life and would like a patient, plain-English guide — the kind of help a kind neighbor might offer, with all the time in the world and none of the sighing.

Why we made this

So much of daily life has quietly moved onto a screen. Doctors send results online, banks close their branches, families share photos through the computer, and forms that used to arrive in the post now live on a website. People who never needed a computer suddenly do, and much of the help on offer is written in a hurry, in jargon, by people who forgot what it was like not to know. We wanted to do the opposite.

How we write our guides

Every guide is written in short steps, in ordinary words, and tested against the way Windows 11 and common email services actually behave. We explain each technical word the first time it appears, we tell you what you should see on screen, and we warn you before anything tricky. Drafts may be prepared with AI assistance and are reviewed and edited by our editorial team before publishing, and every page carries a "Last reviewed" date so you can see how current it is.

What we do not do

Kindly Keystrokes is an independent educational website. That independence matters, so we are clear about our limits. We sell nothing. We offer no repairs, no paid help, and no services of any kind. We will never telephone you, never ask to reach into your computer, and never ask for payment. When your computer needs something only its maker can do, we point you to that company's own official website and show you how to get there safely.

When we mention a company such as Microsoft, Google, or a printer maker, we do so only to teach. Kindly Keystrokes is an independent educational website and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any company mentioned.

Our promise to you

We make a small number of promises and keep them. We will always write in plain English and explain our terms. We will always tell you when a step can go wrong and how to undo it. We will never rush you, never talk down to you, and never pretend a thing is simpler than it is. And we will never, under any circumstances, telephone you, ask to reach into your computer, or ask you for money. Those promises are the whole character of this site.

About the photographs

The photographs on this site are openly licensed images from public collections, downloaded and stored on our own site rather than borrowed from elsewhere on the web. Where a license asks for credit, we name the photographer beneath the picture. A full list of sources and licenses is kept in the site's image credits file.
