Contact Kindly Keystrokes

By the Kindly Keystrokes Editorial Team · Last reviewed: October 2026

We are glad you want to get in touch. The best way to reach us is by email, and we read every message ourselves. There is no form to fill in and no account to create — just write to us as you would a friend.

Email us

Send your note to hello@kindlykeystrokes.com. Tell us which lesson you were reading and what you were hoping to do, and we will do our best to point you to the clearest answer. We aim to reply within two business days.

For a correction to something we have published, you can also write to our editors at editor@kindlykeystrokes.com.

What we can and cannot help with

We are a small team that writes free lessons, so there are limits to what an email can do — and some important things we will never do.

  • We can point you to the right lesson, explain a step more clearly, or fix a mistake on a page.
  • We cannot see your computer, and we would never ask to. We offer no repairs and no paid help.
  • We will never telephone you, never ask for a password, and never ask for payment. Anyone claiming to be us who does these things is an impostor.

A few common questions

Is Kindly Keystrokes really free?
Yes, completely. Every lesson is free to read, there is nothing to buy, and we take no payments of any kind.
Can you fix my computer or log in to help me?
No. We publish reading material only. We have no way to reach your computer and would never ask to. For hands-on repairs, a trusted local shop or the device maker's official website is the right place.
Someone called saying they were from Kindly Keystrokes. Was that you?
No. We never telephone anyone. A call like that is a scam — please hang up, and if you like, report it at reportfraud.ftc.gov.
Will you add more lessons?
We update and expand our guides over time. If there is something you wish we covered, tell us — reader questions shape what we write next.
